## Brand direction

Use warm ivory, sage green, muted coral, and deep charcoal. The visual style should feel clean, friendly, trustworthy, attainable, and premium without appearing clinical.

Prioritize mobile readability, strong headline hierarchy, saveable information, diverse adults, realistic environments, and accessible contrast.

## Safety and quality

Avoid unsupported health promises, body shaming, dangerous form cues, extreme dieting, and medical diagnosis. General wellness content should remain practical and evidence-aware. Encourage professional care when a topic is medical or high risk.
